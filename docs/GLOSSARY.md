# Glossary — useful words, in the context of this code

- **Datum:** a geometric reference (point, axis, plane) from which other features are located. The mounting centers are datums.
- **Graph:** a collection of nodes and connections. Our arm begins as a graph, then becomes a solid.
- **Node:** a named XYZ coordinate used to connect struts and mounts.
- **Strut:** a beam-like volume grown around a straight or curved centerline.
- **Capsule:** the swept volume of a constant-radius sphere along a line segment, equivalently a cylinder with hemispherical ends.
- **Taper:** intentional change in cross-sectional size along a path. The implementation's tapered field is approximate.
- **Quadratic Bézier curve:** a curve defined by two endpoints and one control point; sampling it creates the centerline segments used by the generator.
- **SDF:** signed-distance function. Our convention: negative inside solid, zero at surface, positive outside.
- **Level set / isosurface:** all points with the same field value. `d=0` is the solid boundary.
- **Hard union:** `min(d1,d2)` under the negative-inside sign convention.
- **Smooth union:** an approximate field operation that blends an intersection over a configurable neighborhood.
- **Difference:** subtraction of one solid from another; implemented as `max(solid,-cutter)`.
- **Voxel:** a discrete sampled location/cell in the 3D volume grid, analogous to a 3D pixel.
- **Marching cubes:** algorithm that generates triangles by interpolating an isosurface through a sampled grid.
- **Watertight:** every mesh edge participates in a closed surface. This does not certify dimensions or strength.
- **Topology-inspired:** an organic shape generated by design heuristics or graph rules that *resembles* material-efficient structures.
- **Topology optimization:** physics-based optimization of material distribution within a domain subject to loads, supports, objectives and constraints.
- **B-rep:** boundary representation used by many CAD kernels to store precise analytic/parametric faces and topology.
- **FEA/FEM:** finite element analysis/method, used to compute approximate stresses/displacements given a physical model and boundary conditions.
- **`bpy`:** Blender's Python API, useful for objects, modifiers, rendering and automation; not required to compute the workbench's fields.
